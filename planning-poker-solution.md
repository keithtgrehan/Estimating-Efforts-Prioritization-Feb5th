	•	Visible cards: 1, 2, 3, 5, 8, 13
	Fits Fibronacci Sequence - why?
	•	Effort and uncertainty don’t grow linearly → Fibonacci’s widening gaps reflect real-world risk better than 1–10 scales.
	•	Prevents false precision → stops arguments like “6 vs 7” that add no value.
	•	Forces meaningful discussion → big gaps (e.g. 2 vs 8) can’t be averaged away; assumptions must be surfaced.
	•	Encodes a stop signal → 13 means “too big or too unclear, split it,” not “just estimate higher.”
	•	Keeps estimates relative, not time-based → avoids anchoring to days or hours.
	•	Improves group behaviour → reduces anchoring, seniority bias, and estimation churn.
	•	Maintains discipline → limited card set (1–13) keeps focus on alignment, not math.
